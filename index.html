FAQ

What happens if my subscription ends?

The Pro features switch off and the free plugin keeps working exactly as before, with all your results. Redirects you already made with Pro keep redirecting, so nothing on your site breaks. Renew any time and Pro switches back on.

How does licensing work?

After checkout you get a license key and a download link by email. Install the Pro add-on, paste the key under Tools → Link Checker → License, and you're done. A 1-site key works on one site; the 10-site key on up to ten. To move a key, remove it from the old site first (one click on the License tab), or manage your sites from your Polar customer page.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. Scanning happens on your own server, and there's no account to create. The plugin only contacts the links in your content to see if they work. Pro also checks your license key with Polar once a day, and "Find archived copy" asks the Internet Archive about the one link you clicked.

Will it slow my site down?

Scans run in the background in short batches, never while visitors load pages, and never more than two requests at a time to any one site. On a test site with 2,000 posts and 12,000 links, all content was read in about 20 seconds.

Do I need the free plugin?

Yes. Pro adds to the free plugin, so install the free one from WordPress.org first.

Can I try it first?

The free plugin has no time limit and no link cap. If Pro isn't right for you, the 7-day refund covers you.
